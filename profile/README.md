# 321 AI Labs

321 AI Labs is the research arm of 321ai LLC, an on-prem AI deployment company in Melbourne, Florida. We study the AI systems we and others actually ship — how they fail, how to measure the failure, and what to do about it before it reaches a client.

Our working rule: register the study before we run it, pin every artifact to a hash, and publish what we find. That includes the nulls. A result we can't defend to a skeptic doesn't leave the building as a claim, so we try to make sure it never has to.

## Current public work

**[judge-bias-battery](https://github.com/321-AI-Labs/judge-bias-battery)** — a pre-registered 741-call battery measuring bias in LLM-as-judge panels. All three audited judges flipped verdicts based on answer order and preferred padded answers above chance. The repo ships the harness, the raw data, and a correction model gated on significance tests, so the numbers can be checked rather than trusted.

## What we publish

Across the lab's studies to date: 7 pre-registered studies, a 105-record failure census from production deployment work, classifier agreement of κ 0.965 on our verification pipeline, and null results published as measured. The full study index and rigor stack live at [321ai.xyz/labs](https://321ai.xyz/labs).
